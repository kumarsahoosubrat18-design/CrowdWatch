Four things it does well.

01

Person detection

Pretrained YOLOv8 detects people in configured streams. Throughput and count quality depend on hardware, camera angle, lighting, and crowd overlap.

02

Zone calibration

Select a rectangular or polygon region, enter its measured floor area, and view density estimates from detections inside that zone.

03

Trend forecast

Recent counts estimate crowd levels five and ten minutes ahead to support early warnings. Forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.

04

Volunteer-ready alerts

Configure density and safe-count thresholds, then use pre-alerts to prepare a response before crowd levels rise further.
